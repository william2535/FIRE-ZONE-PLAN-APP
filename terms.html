Zone Sketch is available for a one-off UK purchase price of £1.99. The purchase helps cover developer licence fees and the cost of developing and maintaining the app. There is no subscription. Google Play shows the applicable price and currency before you buy and processes your payment.

Using Zone Sketch for work

You may use Zone Sketch to survey sites, prepare drawings and share project outputs with colleagues and customers. You retain any rights you already hold in the material you import and the drawings you create. Please only import, photograph or share site information you have permission to use.

Checking drawings

Zone Sketch records what you enter. Before issuing a zone plan, device schedule or as-fit drawing, check it against the site and the actual installation. For example, a circuit route drawn on screen does not establish that the installed cable follows that route. Responsibility for verifying the work and signing any required design, commissioning or compliance documents stays with the person issuing them.

Saving your work

Projects are stored on the device. Save an editable backup before replacing a device, clearing app storage or uninstalling Zone Sketch. A PDF or image is useful for sharing a finished drawing. To reopen and edit the project on another device, use the editable backup.
