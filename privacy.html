Zone Sketch Privacy Policy

Google Play edition · Will Flood
Last updated 18 November 2026

Your projects

Zone Sketch is an offline drawing and survey app for fire and security engineers. You can use it without an account. The app saves the information you enter on your Android device, including site names, floor plans, device locations, circuit routes, notes and photos. It uses this information to reopen projects and generate drawings and editable backups. The app does not send projects to Will Flood or upload them to a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising or analytics tools and requests no internet permission.

Photos, files and sharing

You choose which files and photos to import using Android’s file picker or camera. When you export a drawing or backup, you choose where to save it or which app to share it with. An editable backup can include site photos and device locations as well as the drawing. The app or person you send it to can retain a copy.

Purchases and updates

Google Play handles purchase and installation. Zone Sketch does not ask for or receive your card details. Selecting the update option opens Google Play. Google processes information about your use of its store under its Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Projects stay on your device until you delete them. Delete an individual project from its card on the home screen. Clear Zone Sketch’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove all its local records. Delete copies you exported or shared separately wherever you saved or sent them. The developer has no server copy from which to restore a deleted project.

Privacy questions

Contact Will Flood using the developer contact details on the Zone Sketch Google Play listing. You can also raise a general question on the Zone Sketch support page. That page is public. Please keep customer names, addresses, floor plans and security details out of public posts.
