# v0.48 — Circuit Builder reliability and finish

## Confirmed v0.47 failures fixed

- Fast movement skipped devices between pointer events. Segment hit testing now captures crossed devices in travel order.
- Starting a pinch discarded already reached devices from a continuous drag. Reached devices are checkpointed before navigation.
- A different pointer could extend another finger's route. Events are matched to their owning pointer.
- Pointer cancellation left the HUD displaying abandoned progress. Cancellation resets the preview and count together.
- Zoom controls could change the view while unfinished points still used the old pixel coordinates. Navigation checkpoints and ends the drag first.
- Ctrl/Cmd-Z could undo the underlying drawing while Circuit Builder was open. Its keyboard actions now stay within Circuit Builder.

## Additional improvements

- Pinch uses stable pointer IDs; a third finger cannot replace the anchors.
- Resize, app switching and closing Circuit Builder preserve reached devices.
- Canvas redraws are coalesced to one animation frame; symbol lookup uses an index.
- Simplification preserves intentional backtracking. Twin-cable snapping ignores diagonal segments and limits attraction at high zoom.
- Changed or removed surveyed devices block a stale As-Fit. Opening the affected circuit offers a confirmed rebuild; individual circuits can be removed without deleting survey devices.
- Progress no longer obscures the detectors. Final-stretch messages and a completion card show real device/leg counts, with a saved sound toggle and reduced-motion support.
- The completion card remains usable in landscape. The circuit list refreshes immediately after progress, Undo and completion.

## Verification

Run `node tests/run-regressions.cjs` after installing Playwright and Chromium. The runner includes 19 active regression files, including the current Circuit Builder tests; older superseded interaction tests are retained in the repository but are not release gates.

New stress checks cover skipped events, pointer cancellation, pinch handoff, unknown/third pointers, resize, keyboard Undo, repeated loops, reduced motion, sound preference, and phone/landscape reward controls.

The capacity test creates a 120-device / 121-leg loop, checks finite stored geometry, saves to IndexedDB, reloads the page, reopens the route, verifies stale-survey rebuilding, and renders an As-Fit. A local scripted run took about 0.5 seconds; this is a synthetic desktop-browser measurement, not an Android/iPhone performance guarantee.

Twin-cable lane spacing is checked at 1×, 2× and 6×. Real touch and audio behaviour on iOS and Android still benefits from onsite testing.
